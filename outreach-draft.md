## Style rules applied

- No em dash, no en dash, no `X- og Y` coordinated compounds in my own prose. Their
  trading name contains one, so the name is simply not spelled out in the body.
- Plural address throughout (þið / ykkur / ykkar).
- "fyrir ERNU" is the accusative after *fyrir*.
- One person writing, not a company. No unsubscribe line, no legal footer.
- Closes with "Endilega látið mig vita ef þið hafið áhuga", never "kveikir í ykkur".
